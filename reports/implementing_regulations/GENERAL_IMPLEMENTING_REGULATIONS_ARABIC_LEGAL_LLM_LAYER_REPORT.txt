3. LAYER SUMMARY
================================================================================

  This stage creates a structured Arabic Legal LLM-ready JSON layer for the
  95 general implementing-regulation articles and the 4 official forms of the
  Saudi Companies Law (M/132, 1443H).

  The layer is Arabic-only structuring:
    - No translation
    - No English text generated
    - No Chinese text generated
    - No trilingual alignment
    - No public release
    - No paraphrasing or rewriting of official Arabic text
    - official_text_ar preserved verbatim from source intake
    - Article hashes and source provenance preserved

  Each article record includes deterministic metadata for LLM retrieval:
    - record_id (ir-gen-art-001 through ir-gen-art-095)
    - corpus_track (implementing_regulations/general)
    - regulation_scope (general)
    - language (ar)
    - governing_text (arabic_official_source)
    - source_url, source_title, publication dates
    - chapter_number, chapter_title_ar
    - article_number, article_ordinal_ar
    - article_title_ar (extracted deterministically from first line, or null)
    - official_text_ar (verbatim)
    - official_text_hash (SHA-256, matching source intake)
    - legal_status_boundaries
    - source_manifest_hash

  Forms are in a separate JSON file with record_type "official_form".
