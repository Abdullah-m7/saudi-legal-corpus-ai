8. SEARCH BEHAVIOR
================================================================================

  Search fields: text_ar, title_ar, article_ordinal_ar
  Ranking: deterministic lexical scoring
    - Exact phrase in text: +100
    - Exact phrase in title: +50
    - Term in text: +10 per term
    - Term in title: +15 per term
    - All terms in text: +25 bonus
    - All terms in title: +20 bonus
    - Stable tie-break: export_record_id ascending
  Arabic normalization (search only, does not alter stored text):
    - Remove tatweel
    - Normalize alef forms (أ إ آ → ا)
    - Normalize ya (ى → ي)
    - Normalize ta marbuta (ة → ه)
    - Remove diacritics
  No generated index — direct JSONL scan (sufficient for 450 records)
