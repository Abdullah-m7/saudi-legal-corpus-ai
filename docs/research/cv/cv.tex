\section{Research}

\textbf{The Saudi Legal Corpus.} Sole author and maintainer of an open,
machine-readable corpus of Saudi law joining two bodies of material at the
level of the article: 15,855 verified legislative articles across 290
instruments, and 50,666 judgments published in full text by the Ministry of
Justice, of which 15,383 carry the appellate decision in the same record. A
citator links them through 113,052 statutory citations, each matched to the
article cited and carrying the segment of the judgment it sits in, whether the
court or a party is speaking, and whether that judgment survived appeal.

\begin{itemize}
\item Archived on Zenodo, concept DOI
      \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22019182}{10.5281/zenodo.22019182}.
\item Every figure in every manuscript below is computed by deposited code
      from deposited data and typeset from a generated file; checks refuse to
      build a manuscript that types a figure by hand, to use a result older
      than the code that produced it, or to let a note drift from the analysis.
\end{itemize}
