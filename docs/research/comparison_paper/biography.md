# Author biography — a draft for the author to correct

The journal asks for a biography of at most 100 words, in an editable format,
with a passport-type photograph supplied separately.

Written only from facts visible in this repository: what was built, what it
contains, and what has been submitted. Nothing about background, training,
employment or intent, because none of that is in the record and a biography is
a statement about a person rather than about data. **Read it and change
whatever misdescribes you — including the emphasis.**

---

## Draft (97 words)

> Abdullah Almohammedi is an independent researcher working on the

## What was deliberately left out, and why

**Where the papers are under review.** Five manuscripts are with journals now,
none accepted. A biography that lists submissions reads as padding, and a
reviewer who checks will find nothing published.

**"AI-assisted" or any framing of method.** The generative-AI declaration
covers it in full, in its own section, where a reader expects to find it.
Repeating it in a biography invites it to be read as the author's identity
rather than as a disclosure.

**Any claim about being first, or about a gap in the literature.** Neither is
verifiable from this repository, and both are the kind of claim a reviewer
tests first.

**Everything personal.** Degrees, institution, city, career. Not in the record,
so not written by me. Add them yourself if you want them there — most
biographies in this journal carry an affiliation and a field of training.
