# BOOK 4 — TERMINOLOGY LOCK / قفل المصطلحات

Initial locked AR→ZH terminology for شركة المساهمة / 股份公司. Functional legal translations are
proposed; items that are **not yet certain** are marked **`NEEDS_REVIEW`** rather than presented as
final. Terms already locked in the shared glossary (Books 1–3) are marked *(existing)* and must not
be silently changed.

> **Anti-flattening rule.** Do **not** collapse Saudi company-law concepts into PRC Company Law
> (《公司法》) entities where they are only *functionally* comparable. Keep caveats. In particular,
> Saudi **شركة المساهمة** is a Saudi statutory form; `股份公司（JSC）` is a functional label, not an
> assertion of identity with a PRC 股份有限公司.

## Terminology risks to enforce at QA time

- Keep **优先认购权** (Book Four, subscription pre-emption) distinct from Book One **优先购买权**
  (transfer pre-emption) and Book One **赎回权** (redemption).
- Keep **普通股东大会 (OGM)** vs **非常股东大会 (EGM)** vs **类别股东专门大会** strictly separate.
- Keep **种类 (type)** vs **类别 (class)** of shares distinct.
- Keep **已发行资本 (issued)** vs **授权资本 (authorized)** vs **实缴资本 (paid-up)** distinct.
- `NEEDS_REVIEW` items above must be resolved (with the owner / official text) before they are
  written into canonical data.
